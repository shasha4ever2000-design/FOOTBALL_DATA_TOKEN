# Launch video plan: Ahmed Hussein · AI Lessons

**What it is:** Free, short AI lessons in plain English and Arabic, made by an accountant in Riyadh.
**Who it's for:** Office and finance people who want to use ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot at work without the hype.
**What sets it apart:** It's bilingual, every lesson is under ten minutes, and you don't need to sign up. It's built around a mashrabiya idea: AI softened into light you can work in.
**Most impressive claim:** 19 lessons, 2 languages, 30 prompts ready to copy, 0 sign-ups needed.
**Visual hook:** A dark room. As a prompt gets built, the lattice window fills with light.
**Real UI to show:** The prompt builder (Role, Task, Context, Format, then the meter hits 100%), the three courtyard cards, the stats row, the "Hi, I'm Ahmed" portrait.
**Tone:** `polished` but warm. Long holds and soft dips through the background. Same cream, amber and brown palette as the site.
**Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Angle
Ahmed's own line: "Most people don't need more AI news. They need clear steps." We open in the dark and let the site's light metaphor carry us through.

## Visual identity
- Background `#F4EEE6`, surface `#FCF9F4`, text `#1F160F`, accent `#F2A33A`, dark room `#16100C`
- Prompt colors: role `#9A5800`, task `#16756B`, context `#B0402B`, format `#3A62B0`
- Fonts: El Messiri (headings), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (body), IBM Plex Mono (small labels)
- Art: the site's own inline SVG courtyard lattices (amber, teal, blue), the star brand mark, and the portrait (cropped from a live screenshot)

## Storyboard (21s, 1920×1080, 30fps)
| # | Time | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–3.4s | **Hook.** Dark room. "Most people don't need more AI news." Then, in amber: "They need clear steps." |
| 2 | 3.4–7.4s | **Reveal.** Light floods in: the amber lattice arch glows and stars spill across the floor. Eyebrow "From an accountant in Riyadh", headline "AI, softened into light you can work in." |
| 3 | 7.4–12.6s | **Highlight 1: prompt builder.** The real builder: Role, Task, Context and Format type in one at a time. Each star pops, the meter climbs 25 → 100%, the colored prompt builds up, then "Copy prompt" gets pressed. Heading: "Build a prompt. Let the light in." |
| 4 | 12.6–15.6s | **Highlight 2: courtyards.** Three lattice cards rise in: AI for work (11), Finance & CMA (2), Microsoft 365 (6). Heading: "Three courtyards, 19 lessons." |
| 5 | 15.6–18.0s | **Highlight 3: numbers.** Stats count up: 19 short lessons · 2 languages · 30 prompts · 0 sign-ups needed. The Arabic headline appears under them. |

## Sound
A warm D-major pad that opens up at the reveal. Soft plucks in key mark each prompt part landing, and a low swell plus chime plays on 100%. It all ends on a gentle resolved chord.

## Notes

- No personal data is shown beyond what the public site already shows (Ahmed's name and photo). The WhatsApp number and social links are left out.
