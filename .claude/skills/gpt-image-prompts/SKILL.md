# GPT Image Prompts

A curated set of prompts that are known to work well with GPT Image 2. The full library is in `references/prompt-library.md`, grouped into these sections:

- 📷 Photography & Photorealism
- 🎮 Game & Entertainment
- 📱 UI/UX & Social Media
- 🎬 Video, Animation & Collage
- 📰 Typography & Poster Design
- 📚 Infographics, Education & Documents
- 🎭 Character & Consistency
- 🖼️ Image Editing & Style Transfer

## How to use

1. Work out what kind of image the user wants and which section fits.
2. Search `references/prompt-library.md` for that section (grep the `## ` and `### ` headings) instead of reading the whole file.
3. Pick the one to three closest prompts and adapt them to the user's subject. Keep what makes them work: the concrete camera/lighting/style details, the layout rules, and the "avoid" lists.
4. Hand back a ready-to-paste prompt. Mention which library prompt it was based on and link its source.

## Tips that show up across the library

- Be specific about the medium: "RAW iPhone photo", "in-game screenshot with HUD", "direct flash", "90s point-and-shoot".
- For posters and infographics, spell out the layout, text blocks, and colour palette as a list.
- For consistency across panels or edits, say what must stay the same ("face, hairstyle and clothing stay identical").
- For edits, say what to change and what to keep ("keep composition and details").

The library is community-sourced. Some prompts name real people or brands; don't use those to make misleading or harmful images.
